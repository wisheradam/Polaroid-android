# Film geometry references

Accessed 9 November 2026. Dimensions below are **width × height**, in millimetres. Millimetres define ratios, not physical pixels on a phone. The simulation always supplies ten exposures even where contemporary real packs use a different count.

| Format | Exterior | Image | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classic | 88.47 × 107.52 | 76.801 × 78.94 | Polaroid Support dimension article |
| Go | 53.9 × 66.6 | 46 × 47 | Same manufacturer article |
| Spectra | 103.124 × 101.6 | 91.44 × 73.66 | Spectra Pro manufacturer manual, specifications |
| Round | 88.47 × 107.52 | 76.81 diameter | Same manufacturer article |

Manufacturer article: https://support.polaroid.com/hc/en-us/articles/115012363647-What-are-Polaroid-photo-dimensions

Spectra Pro manual: https://support.polaroid.com/hc/en-us/article_attachments/360003014727

Classic image is near-square using the manufacturer's explicit inch-to-centimetre conversion. Spectra dimensions convert the manual's 4 × 4.06 inch exterior (portrait height first) and 3.60 × 2.90 inch landscape image. Border top inset is a deliberate visual approximation, because the references specify image/exterior sizes rather than every border offset. The bottom margin is larger than the top on all formats. Circular area uses the real stated round-frame diameter.
